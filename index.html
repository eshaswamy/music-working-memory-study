Study: Music, Mood & Working Memory in High-School Students

Your child is being invited to participate in an independent student research project. The study includes brief questionnaires about mood, anxiety, and music habits and two short computer working-memory blocks, one with self-selected music and one in silence.

Participation is voluntary. The research questionnaire does not collect the participant's name, email, school name, exact date of birth, or student ID. The permission record is stored separately from research responses.

Some questions ask about sadness, worry, sleep, concentration, and energy. The questionnaires are research measures, not diagnoses, and individual scores are not shown to participants.

Research contact: Esha Swamy (eshaswamy@icloud.com)

Data/withdrawal policy: Participation is voluntary. Your child may stop before the final study submission without penalty. Student research responses are stored without names, email addresses, school names, exact birth dates, or student IDs. Parent/guardian permission records are stored separately from research responses and are not linked to the anonymous research dataset. After the student submits the study, the anonymous research response generally cannot be withdrawn because there is no identifying link that would allow the researcher to locate a specific response. A parent/guardian may contact Esha Swamy at eshaswamy@icloud.com to ask questions or request deletion of the separate parent-permission record. Research data will be kept in a password-protected Google account, accessed only for this project and any required supervision, and reported only in summary or aggregate form. Data will be deleted no later than one year after the project is completed.
